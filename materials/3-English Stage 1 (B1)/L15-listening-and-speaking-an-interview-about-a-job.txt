## Before You Watch
## Discuss
You're going to watch a video interview about Andy. Before you watch, discuss the following questions in pairs or groups of three.
1. These are pictures of Andy. He is a farmer. What do you think he does every day?
2. Andy also cares a lot about the environment. Because of this, he practices sustainable agriculture. What is sustainable agriculture? If you don't know, you can guess.
3. In the video, Andy will talk about his values (the things that are most important to him in life). What are your values?
Figure: Images by Brittany Zemlick
## Vocabulary
### Vocabulary Task 1
Do you know these words? Discuss them with your partner. Then share what you know with the class. Use the pictures below to help you understand the ideas.
- sustainable
- sustainable agriculture
- industrial agriculture
- values
(Picture: two windmills in a field) (Picture: two brown and white cows in a pasture) (Picture: A family picture with a mom, a dad, and four young children.)
Figure: Images by __Demiahl__ from __Pixabay, Photo Mix__ from __Pixabay,__ Craig Cooper on Unsplash, and Simbarashe Takawira on Unsplash.
Notes:
- Windmills and solar panels are an example of sustainable energy sources because wind and sun cannot be used up completely or destroyed.
- Industrial agriculture is a type of farming that involves growing large numbers of plants and animals to be sold for food.
- Your values are the things that are most important to you. They can include family, money, education, music, art, etc. What is most important to you in life?
