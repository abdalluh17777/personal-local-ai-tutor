# Lesson 26: Listening and Speaking: A Zero Waste How-To Video

*English Stage 1 (B1) · Unit 3: Zero Waste*
*Adapted from Speaking, Listening and Pronunciation Projects for ELLs - Intermediate Level by Brittany Zemlick, Grossmont College (CC BY 4.0): 3.1 Listening and Speaking - Zero Waste "How To" Video; 3.2 Vocabulary Review.*

## In this lesson
You will think about trash and how to make less of it. Then you will watch a short "how to" video in which Lauren Singer makes her own zero waste toothpaste. You will learn the words from the video and use them in sentences.

## Learning goals
After this lesson you should be able to:
- Talk about trash and give tips to reduce it.
- Understand the main idea and the details of a "how to" video.
- Use the unit words (recycle, upcycle, recipe, alternative, combine, stir and more).
- Name the genre, audience and purpose of a video.
- Give your opinion about making your own products.

## Key language
| Word or phrase | Meaning in simple English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| zero waste | making very little or no trash | Lauren Singer lives a Zero Waste lifestyle. |
| recycle | send used paper, plastic or glass to be made into new things | Toothpaste tubes are difficult to recycle. |
| upcycle | find a new way to use something instead of throwing it in the trash | Lauren upcycled a chopstick. |
| reusable | you can use it again and again (opposite: disposable) | Take a reusable grocery bag when you go shopping. |
| recipe | a list of instructions and ingredients for making food | Would you try making the recipe in the video? |
| alternative | another option | We can use wind energy as an alternative to coal. |
| combine | add together | Combine coconut oil, baking soda and essential oils. |
| stir | mix | Lauren uses a chopstick to stir the toothpaste. |
| consistency | how hard, soft or liquid something is | The consistency might change because of temperature. |
| fluctuate | change a lot | The consistency of the toothpaste fluctuates. |
| foam up | turn white and make lots of bubbles | Toothpaste from the store usually foams up. |

## Rules and explanations

### Zero waste
- "Trash" and "waste" mean the same thing: the things we throw away.
- Lauren Singer produces very little or no trash. All of her trash from the past 5 years can fit in one mason jar (a glass jar). One way she reduces her waste is by making her own products, like toothpaste.

### Recycle, upcycle, reusable
- **Recycle:** you put plastic, paper or glass in a recycling bin, and it is made into something new.
- **Upcycle:** you find a new use for an old thing yourself.
- **Reusable** things (a bag, cutlery) are used many times. **Disposable** things are used once and thrown away.

### Recipe words
- You **combine** the ingredients, **stir** them and measure with a **tablespoon**. Essential oils are natural oils that have different flavors and smells.
- Coconut oil is hard when it's cold and liquid when it's hot, so the consistency **fluctuates**.

### Listening to a "how to" video
- Watch first for the **main idea**, then again for **details** (ingredients, tools, steps, tips).
- Then think about its **genre** (the type of video), **audience** (the people it is for) and **purpose** (why it was made).

> **Note for Arabic speakers**
> - Arabic has no /p/ sound, so /p/ can sound like /b/. This lesson has many /p/ words: peppermint, recipe, toothpaste, upcycle. Say /p/ as in "pen" with a small puff of air; say /b/ as in "baking" with your voice.

## Practice

**1. Before you watch.** The book shows two photos of beaches covered in trash. Think of a beach or street you know with trash, and answer aloud:
- What do you see? Why is there so much trash? Where does it come from?
- What do you think is the solution? How can you reduce the amount of trash you produce?

**2. Lauren Singer.** How do you think she makes so little trash? Watch the first 27 seconds of the first video (see Watch online). What did you learn about Lauren Singer?

**3. Zero waste tips.** The book has photos of (A) toothbrushes in a jar, (B) a reusable shopping bag, (C) glass jars full of dry food such as pasta, grains and coffee. Match each photo with a tip:
- a. use a bamboo toothbrush (not plastic)
- b. take a reusable grocery bag when you go shopping
- c. bring your own containers when you go shopping
- d. use reusable cutlery (not disposable)

Then answer: Do you already do some of these things? Which new tip would you like to try? Why?
