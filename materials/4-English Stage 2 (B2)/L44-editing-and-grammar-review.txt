# Unit 20 Editing / Grammar Review
(Picture: a typewriter typing "rewrite...edit...")
a typewriter typing “rewrite…edit…”
This unit aims to give you a broad review of the editing / grammar skills covered in this book. You may find the corresponding information from Unit 7 through Unit 19.
The materials in this unit are adapted from the work written by former ESL intermediate level (Writing III) students at Harper College. The errors represent typical mistakes in student writing. Anything that identifies the student writers, such as their names and native countries, has been removed.
# **I. Suggestions for Completing the Unit**
1. Unlike those in previous units, the sentences in this unit are unrelated. Therefore, you can work on each sentence individually without worrying about the context.
2. You may do the exercises under each topic as you finish the related editing / grammar unit(s) in the previous part of this book. For example, you can do “Review Sentence Structure and Punctuation” after completing Unit 7 Sentence Essentials and Unit 8 Three Common Errors in Sentence Structure and Punctuation. The links to the related editing units are provided.
3. You may also do all or some of the parts in this unit before your major tests. However, allow yourself enough time to finish the exercises and to study your weak areas if necessary.
4. As you work though Unit 20, grow more mindful of the common errors and ways to fix them. Try to avoid those types of errors in your writing. In fact, the best approach to improvement is through writing and then editing your own writing.

[Interactive exercise]
Q: 1. Check the correct combined sentence(s). I have a lot of writing homework. I have a reading project due. (also)
   Options: I have a lot of writing homework, also I have a reading project due. | I have a lot of writing homework, I also have a reading project due. | I have a lot of writing homework. Also, I have a reading project due. | I have a lot of writing homework, also, I have a reading project due. | I have a lot of writing homework; also, I have a reading project due.
   Answer: I have a lot of writing homework. Also, I have a reading project due. | I have a lot of writing homework; also, I have a reading project due.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 2. Check the correct combined sentence(s). I saw the horse. I was surprised and happy. (when)
   Options: When I saw the horse I was surprised and happy. | When I saw the horse; I was surprised and happy. | When I saw the horse, I was surprised and happy. | When I saw the horse. I was surprised and happy.
   Answer: When I saw the horse, I was surprised and happy.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 3. Check the correct combined sentence(s). The goldfish is very beautiful. It is hard to feed. (but)
   Options: The goldfish is very beautiful, but it is hard to feed. | The goldfish is very beautiful, but, it is hard to feed. | The goldfish is very beautiful but it is hard to feed. | The goldfish is very beautiful. But it is hard to feed.
   Answer: The goldfish is very beautiful, but it is hard to feed.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 4. Check the correct combined sentence(s). Service dogs are helpful. They can help blind people on the street. (because)
   Options: Service dogs are helpful. Because they can help blind people on the street. | Because they can help blind people on the street, service dogs are helpful. | Service dogs are helpful because they can help blind people on the street. | Service dogs are helpful because, they can help blind people on the street.
   Answer: Because they can help blind people on the street, service dogs are helpful. | Service dogs are helpful because they can help blind people on the street.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 5. Check the correct combined sentence(s). It was windy. We finished our daily walk. (although)
   Options: Although, it was windy, we finished our daily walk. | Although it was windy, we finished our daily walk. | We finished our daily walk although it was windy. | It was windy; although; we finished our daily walk. (although)
   Answer: Although it was windy, we finished our daily walk. | We finished our daily walk although it was windy.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 6. Check the correct combined sentence(s). My sister does not like sports. She does not like gardening. (nor)
   Options: My sister does not like sports, nor she does not like gardening. | My sister does not like sports, nor does she like gardening. | My sister does not like sports, nor she likes gardening. | My sister does not like sports, nor she not like gardening.
   Answer: My sister does not like sports, nor does she like gardening.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 7. Check the correct combined sentence(s). I have the opportunity to go there. I will gladly go. (as soon as)
   Options: As soon as I have the opportunity to go there, I will gladly go. | I will gladly go; as soon as, I have the opportunity to go there. | I will gladly go as soon as I have the opportunity to go there. | As soon as, I have the opportunity to go there, I will gladly go.
   Answer: As soon as I have the opportunity to go there, I will gladly go. | I will gladly go as soon as I have the opportunity to go there.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 8. Check the correct combined sentence(s). I worked hard to prepare for the last test. I get an A! (as a result)
   Options: I worked hard to prepare for the last test as a result I get an A! | I worked hard to prepare for the last test, as a result I get an A! | I worked hard to prepare for the last test; as a result I get an A! | I worked hard to prepare for the last test; as a result, I get an A!
   Answer: I worked hard to prepare for the last test; as a result, I get an A!
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 9. Check the correct combined sentence(s). Chicago is a wonderful city. It is too cold in winter. (however)
   Options: However Chicago is a wonderful city, it is too cold in winter. | Chicago is a wonderful city, however, It is too cold in winter. | Chicago is a wonderful city; however, it is too cold in winter. | Chicago is a wonderful city however it is too cold in winter.
   Answer: Chicago is a wonderful city; however, it is too cold in winter.
[Interactive exercise]
Q: 10. Check the correct combined sentence(s). My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict. I had to change one of them. (so)
   Options: My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict. So, I had to change one of them. | My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict so I had to change one of them. | My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict so, I had to change one of them. | My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict, so I had to change one of them.
   Answer: My class schedule and work schedule had a conflict, so I had to change one of them.
