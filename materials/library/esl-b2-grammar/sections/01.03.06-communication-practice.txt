## 1.3.6.1. **Exercise 11-Chain Stories**
The sentence below is the beginning of a story. Each member of your group should add two sentences to the story using at least one phrasal verb. The story can be shocking, funny, wonderful, or embarrassing, but it is a story that should make sense. Be creative!
First sentence of the story: Yesterday began like any other day.
