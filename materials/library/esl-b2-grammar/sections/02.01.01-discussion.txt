5.1: Discussion

Answer the following questions based on your experience and opinion. Read five classmates’ posts and respond to two classmates pointing out any similarities or differences, asking clarifying questions, or giving your opinion on a point they make.
1. Think about the countries around the world. Who is indigenous to those lands? Who occupied those lands? Provide examples.
2. Think about the groups of people in the United States. How did the US become so diverse?
3. Are you familiar with the history of how minoritized groups in the US (e.g., Indigenous people, African Americans, Asian Americans, Muslims, Sikhs, immigrants, refugees, etc.) have been treated? Explain.
