Answer the following questions based on your experience and opinion. Read five classmates’ posts and respond to two classmates pointing out any similarities or differences, asking clarifying questions, or giving your opinion on a point they make.
1. What can help children’s brain develop? How can parents help children’s brains develop?
2. Can technology be beneficial for children? How? Why not?
