## 1.2.2.1. **Exercise 1-Listening Comprehension**
Watch the video __5 Unhealthy Coping Mechanisms You Shouldn’t Ignore__ and build a basic understanding to prepare you for this chapter’s reading. Answer the questions to check your comprehension.
1. Which of the following mechanisms is an unhealthy way to deal with stress?
  a. exercise
  b. listening to music
  c. staying positive about all situations
2. Which of the following is not an example of toxic positivity?
  a. “Don’t think about it, be positive!”
  b. “Everything will work out in the end.”
  c. “If I can do it, so can you.”
  d. “It could be worse.”
  e. “It’s OK to feel bad sometimes.”
3. What is a healthy way to deal with social anxiety?
  a. Force yourself to attend parties.
  b. Isolate in your room.
  c. Invite someone you feel comfortable with to accompany you in social situations.
  d. Use social media to make friends.
4. The fourth unhealthy coping mechanism relates to repressing emotions. However, it is possible to overreact. Which strategy to minimize emotion was not provided?
  a. yelling into a pillow
  b. meditating
  c. dancing
  d. talking on the phone
