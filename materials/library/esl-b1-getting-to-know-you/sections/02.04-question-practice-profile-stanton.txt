## Watching a Video Profile
## Brandon Stanton
[Video: https://www.youtube.com/embed/Bcm6kwWv09o]
## Video Preview
Watch the video profile about Brandon Stanton. After you watch the video one time, write a few keywords or sentences in your notes. What surprised or interested you about the video? Discuss with your classmates.
## Watch Again
Watch the video a second time. This time, imagine that you are the journalist writing a profile of Brandon Stanton. What questions should you ask him to find out more information about his passions? Interviewers should always ask **open-ended** questions. Open-ended questions begin with a "wh" question word and allow for a long answer. Stop the video at the suggested points below and take a few minutes to write down an **open-ended** question for Brandon. The first question has been written for you as an example.
Open-ended question words are ***what, when, where, why, who, how.*** Try to start your question with one of these words.
Example: What do you love about your job?
