## Watching a TED Talk
## Celeste Headlee "10 Ways to Have a Better Conversation"
Celeste Headlee is an experienced radio host who interviews people for a living. Watch __the video__ and then answer the questions below.
[Interactive exercise: https://embed.ted.com/talks/celeste_headlee_10_ways_to_have_a_better_conversation]
- What skill does Celeste Headlee think we need to learn?
- What problems make conversation difficult these days?
- As you hear them in the TED talk, put a check next to the rules for better conversation. Take notes. Be prepared to explain the rules to your classmates.
  - Don't multitask
  - Don't pontificate
  - Ask open-ended questions
  - Go with the flow
  - If you don't know, say you don't know
  - Don't equate your experiences with others
  - Don't repeat yourself
  - Stay out of the weeds
  - Listen
  - Be brief
- According to Celeste Headlee, which rule is the most important?
- In your opinion, which rule is the most important? Do you disagree with any rule? Can you add a rule?
- What does Celeste Headlee mean when she says "Be prepared to be amazed"?
- Remember the video. Which features did the TED talk have? Identify all that apply.
  - Quotes
  - Jokes
  - Visuals
  - Examples
  - A list
- When you have a conversation, what is the best habit you have to make it a good conversation?
