## Watch Brandon Stanton in Action
Brandon Stanton is a photographer in New York who interviews and writes stories about the strangers he photographs. He has a famous website, Facebook page, and book called Humans of New York (HONY). Watch __his video__ and consider the questions below.
[Video: https://www.youtube.com/embed/8EPUveE04_c]
- What kind of questions does Brandon ask when he interviews a stranger?
- What do you notice about his attitude toward the people he interviews?
