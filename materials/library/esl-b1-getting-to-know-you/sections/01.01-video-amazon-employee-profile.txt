## Analyzing a Profile Video
**Watch the employee profile video and answer the questions below. Then discuss with your instructor and classmates.**
[Video: https://www.youtube.com/embed/IoyvKtwXqeA]
- Where would you expect to see this video? (**genre)**
  - On Instagram
  - On a dating website
  - On a college website
  - On a company website
- Who is the **audience** for this video? Choose all that apply.
  - The company Amazon
  - The general public
  - John's friends and family
  - A company that needs employees
- What is the **purpose** of this video? Choose all that apply.
  - To help John find a new job
  - To explain step by step how to get hired at Amazon
  - To show how happy an employee is working for Amazon
  - To make the company feel more personal by introducing some of its employees
- What elements of the reading helped you decide the answers to the questions above? Choose all that apply.
  - The topic
  - The format
  - The register (formal or informal language)
  - The pictures
  - The vocabulary
  - Other
**This is a video of an employee profile. From watching this video, what are some of the features of a profile? Choose the best answer to each question.**
- A profile is:
  - About one person
  - About many people
- A profile is:
  - Short
  - Long
- A profile has:
  - A person's whole life history
  - A few facts about the person's history
- A profile focuses on:
  - One part of a person's life, such as work, school, a hobby, or a special interest
  - Everything that is important to that person
- A profile has:
  - A negative tone
  - A positive tone
- A profile is about:
  - Someone famous
  - Anyone
An answer key is attached, but answers may vary.
