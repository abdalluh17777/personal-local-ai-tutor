## Practice: -S Endings
Does the final sound of each word have an /s/ sound or a /z/ sound?
### Write the words in the correct columns of the table below
recipes makes alternatives chopsticks combines tablespoons takes benefits
coconuts liquids oils foams fluctuates wastes stirs forks ingredients
Write the words above in the correct column.
| /s/ | /z/ |
| EXAMPLE: recipes |
