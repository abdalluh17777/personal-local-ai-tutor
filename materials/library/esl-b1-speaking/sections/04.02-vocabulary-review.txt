## Vocabulary Review 2: Sentence Completion
Using new vocabulary words in sentences will help you remember the new words and it will help you practice your spelling. Use the words below to complete each sentence. Use the definitions from your Quizlet flashcards to help you answer the questions. You must spell the words correctly.
### Query 
[Interactive exercise]
Task: Fill in the missing words
   A. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. fidget concentrate learning disorder invented radio ballet tap soloist musical theater 1. Young children often [fidget] in school. They move around a lot and they find it difficult to sit still. 2. Children who have a [learning disorder] often have problems with learning. 3. Please stop making so much noise. I’m trying to do my homework and I can’t [concentrate] . 4. [Musical theater] is a kind of performance where people sing and dance on stage. 5. The [soloist] in the ballet did a beautiful dance. 6. Thomas Edison [invented] the lightbulb. I was the first one to make a long-lasting electric light bulb. 7. I like all kinds of dance including [ballet], and [tap]. 8. I listen to the [radio] a lot while I’m driving. Sometimes I listen to music, but usually I listen to the news.
   B. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. multimillionaire calm down medication ADHD inventor inventions founded a specialist 1. Gillian’s mother took her to see a [specialist]. They want to see this doctor because they thought Gillian had a learning disorder. 2. Gillian Lynne was a very successful dancer and choreographer. She earned a lot of money doing her work and she became a [multimillionaire]. 3. Gillian Lynne [founded] the Gillian Lynne Dance Company. That means she started the company. 4. Sometimes my children have too much energy and I have to tell them to [calm down]. 5. [ADHD/adhd] is a learning disorder. Children with this disorder have to move around a lot, and they have problems concentrating and learning. 6. The doctor gives me [medication] when I’m sick. 7. Alexander Graham Bell was a famous [inventor]. He invented the telephone. 8. People have made many new [inventions] in the last 100 years such as antibiotics, the TV, the smart phone, and the electric car.
