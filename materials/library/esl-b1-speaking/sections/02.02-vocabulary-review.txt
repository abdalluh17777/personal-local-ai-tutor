## Vocabulary Review 2: Sentence Completion
Using new vocabulary words in sentences will help you remember the new words and it will help you practice your spelling. Use the words below to complete each senescence. Use the definitions from your Quizlet flashcards to help you answer the questions. You must spell the words correctly.
### Query 
[Interactive exercise]
Task: Fill in the missing words
   A. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. sustainable industrial agriculture values plant crops pull weeds water harvest pickup truck repair produce 1. I need to [water] the plants in the garden. It’s very dry and hot today. 2. Andy drives a [pickup truck]. He uses it for his farming business. 3. Many people buy [produce] from the grocery store, but you can also grow fruits and vegetables at home. 4. Wind energy is [sustainable] because we can use it forever and it will never run out. Coal energy is not. 5. Farmers usually [plant crops] in the spring. This means, they put seeds in the ground and wait for them to grow into food. 6. Farmers usually [harvest] crops in the fall. This means, they pick the fruits and vegetables and then eat them or sell them. 7. In the video, Andy talks about his [values]. The most important things in his life are family and protecting the environment. 8. If you [repair] something, that means you’re fixing it. 9. [Industrial agriculture/industrial agriculture] means growing large amounts of vegetables, fruit, and animals, usually in a relatively small space. This kind of farming can damage the environment, so we won’t be able to grow food in the future. 10. Gardeners [pull weeds] (remove plants they don’t want) to make room for the plants they want to grow.
   B. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. podcasts challenging vegan environmental impact meat production pollutes carbon habitats fences 1. A [vegan] is a person who doesn’t eat meat or use animal products. 2. Andy says working with the public can be [challenging]. That’s a nice way to say that working with people can sometimes be difficult. 3. I like to listen to [podcasts] on my iphone. There are a lot of interesting news and story programs like Fresh Air and The Moth. 4. Industrial agriculture is bad for the environment because it [pollutes] waterways. That means it makes the water dirty. 5. If we destroy the [habitats] of animals, they won’t have a place to live. 6. Industrial agriculture and some forms of energy production release [carbon] into the atmosphere. This can cause the climate to heat up and adds to global warming. 7. [Meat production/meat production] is the process of raising animals to use them for food. 8. Many people are concerned about the [environmental impact] of airplanes. They can cause a lot of pollution. 9. Farmers have to put [fences] around their crops so that animals won’t eat them.
