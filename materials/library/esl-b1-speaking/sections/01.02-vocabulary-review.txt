## Vocabulary Review 3: Sentence Completion
Using new vocabulary words in sentences will help you remember the new words, and it will help you practice your spelling. Use the words below to complete each sentence. Use the definitions from your "25 Things to do in Berlin" worksheet and your Quizlet flashcards to help you answer the questions. You must spell the words correctly.
### Query 
[Interactive exercise]
Task: Fill in the missing words
   A. Use the words below to complete the sentences. vintage vinyls flea market art galleries hangout exudes to spot neighborhood wild deer symbol 1. I like to shop at the [flea market] because it’s easy to find cheap clothes, shoes and furniture. 2. We went to Hollywood, and we tried [to spot] celebrities. 3. I live in a quiet [neighborhood] with lots of pretty houses. Unfortunately, I don’t know many of my neighbors. 4. I like to go to [art galleries] to look at the paintings. 5. The US American flag is an important [symbol] of freedom. 6. The princess [exudes] elegance and confidence. 7. Sometimes people like to hunt [wild deer]. They like the sport of shooting animals, and they eat the meat they kill. 8. Some people think the mall is a fun [hangout] spot. They like to spend time there. 9. I like to wear [vintage] clothing. I think old-style clothing is cool. 10. We used to have lots of old [vinyls] and a record player, but now we have a CD player.
   B. Use the words below to complete the sentences. vibe curry powder concoction sausage dangling memorial graffiti infamous dates back to green escape 1. I’m getting a bad [vibe] from these people. I don’t like the feeling I have when I’m around them. 2. I don’t eat [sausage]. I’m a vegetarian. 3. Our dessert last night was a peanut butter and chocolate [concoction]. It looked like a strange mixture, but it tasted delicious. 4. I eat a lot of [curry powder]. I love spicy foods! 5. The Berlin Wall is a [memorial] that helps us remember the Cold War. The Cold War was a difficult time when East and West Berlin were separated, and East Berlin was controlled by the Soviet Union. 6. The Berlin Wall is covered in colorful [graffiti], or street art. 7. Hitler was [infamous]. He was famous for doing very bad things. 8. Micheal Jackson is infamous for [dangling] his baby out of a winder in a hotel in Berlin. 9. The Washington Monument [dates back to] the 1840s. It was built in honor of George Washington, the first US president. 10. Central Park is a nice quiet [green escape] from the business and pollution of New York City.
