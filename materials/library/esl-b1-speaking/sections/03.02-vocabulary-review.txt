## Vocabulary Review 2: Sentence Completion
Using new vocabulary words in sentences will help you remember the new words and it will help you practice your spelling. Use the words below to complete each sentence. Use the definitions from your Quizlet flashcards to help you answer the questions. You must spell the words correctly.
### Query 
[Interactive exercise]
Task: Fill in the missing words
   A. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. toothpaste recipe alternative recycle combine coconut oil liquids head to tablespoon 1. I brush my teeth twice a day, and I use a toothbrush and [toothpaste]. 2. It’s always a good idea to [recycle] plastic and paper; however, it’s even better when you don’t make any trash to begin with. 3. The recipe says to [combine] two eggs, flower and sugar in a bowl. 4. Then you need to add one [tablespoon] of coco powder. 5. A [recipe] is a set of instructions for making food; however, in this video the woman uses a list of ingredients and instructions to make toothpaste (something we don’t eat). 6. We need to [head to] the store. I need to buy some fruit and vegetables. 7. Making your own products like toothpaste and lotion is a good [alternative] to buying them. 8. [Coconut oil/coconut oil] is a vegan alternative to butter. 9. Water, milk, and orange juice are examples of [liquids].
   B. Use the words below to complete the sentences. You must spell the words correctly. baking soda peppermint cinnamon chopstick upcycle foams up consistency fluctuates zero waste 1. Toothpaste usually [foams up] in your mouth when you brush your teeth. That means the toothpaste turns white and makes lots of bubbles. 2. When you [upcycle] something that means you find a new way to use it instead of throwing it in the trash. 3. If you lead a [zero waste] lifestyle, that means you don’t make any trash. 4. A [chopstick] is a long wooden stick that people sometimes use instead of a fork to eat food. In the video, Lauren upcycled a [chopstick] and she used it to stir the toothpaste. (Use the same word for both blanks.) 5. When something [fluctuates] that means it changes a lot. In the video, Lauren said the consistency of the toothpaste can change a lot. When it’s cold, the toothpaste gets hard and when it’s hot, the toothpaste can become liquid. 6. [Cinnamon/cinnamon/peppermint/Peppermint] and [peppermint/cinnamon] are two examples of flavors you can put in your toothpaste. 7. The [consistency] of butter and coconut oil can change. When they are hot, they are liquid and when they are cold, they become solid. 8. I use [baking soda] in cookie and cake recipes, but you can also use it in your toothpaste recipe.
