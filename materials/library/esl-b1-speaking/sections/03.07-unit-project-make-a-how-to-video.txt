## Task
1. You are going to make a video that teaches your classmates how to do something. You can teach the class how to do anything that interests you. Try to pick something you think other students would be interested in learning about.
2. Include the following:
  - Give an introduction: Explain what you will teach the class to do.
  - Explain why this skill is important, helpful or interesting for your classmates to learn.
  - Your instruction video must include at least 3 steps.
  - Make a video of yourself doing the task and explaining each of the steps.
  - Conclusion: End with some final tips and/or summary of your presentation.
  - Include a transcript: Write down what you're doing to say. Circle the content words in each sentence. Draw lines between words that are linked. Practice reading your transcript with the correct pronunciation.
## Possible topics:
How to . . .
- make the perfect cup of coffee.
- change a bicycle tire.
- post on Instagram.
- make a TikTok video.
- make or do something from your country or culture.
## Purpose: Why are we doing this?
- You will practice speech giving skills including volume, eye contact, organization and presenting information about a place you know well.
- You will review and practice using pronunciation features we have learned in class including stress and rhythm when saying content words and function words as well as linking in connected speech.
