## Before You Watch
## Discuss
You're going to watch a video interview about Andy. Before you watch, discuss the following questions in pairs or groups of three.
1. These are pictures of Andy. He is a farmer. What do you think he does every day?
2. Andy also cares a lot about the environment. Because of this, he practices sustainable agriculture. What is sustainable agriculture? If you don't know, you can guess.
3. In the video, Andy will talk about his values (the things that are most important to him in life). What are your values?

## Vocabulary
### Vocabulary Task 1
Do you know these words? Discuss them with your partner. Then share what you know with the class. Use the pictures below to help you understand the ideas.
- sustainable
- sustainable agriculture
- industrial agriculture
- values
[Image: two windmills in a field] [Image: two brown and white cows in a pasture] [Image: craig-cooper-mADanQnJ_oU-unsplash.jpg] [Image: A family picture with a mom, a dad, and four young children.]
Figure : Images by __Demiahl__ from __Pixabay, Photo Mix__ from __Pixabay,__ Craig Cooper on Unsplash, and Simbarashe Takawira on Unsplash.
Notes:
- Windmills and solar panels are an example of sustainable energy sources because wind and sun cannot be used up completely or destroyed.
- Industrial agriculture is a type of farming that involves growing large numbers of plants and animals to be sold for food.
- Your values are the things that are most important to you. They can include family, money, education, music, art, etc. What is most important to you in life?

## Listen for the Main Idea
While you watch, answer the questions below. Takes notes. Then share your answers with your partner.
1. What does Andy do every day in his job?
2. What are his values?

## After You Watch
## Post-Listening Discussion
Discuss and answer these questions with a partner. Be ready to explain ***why*** you chose each answer.
1. What is the genre of this video?
  - a TikTok video
  - a movie
  - a presentation
  - an interview
2. Who is the most likely audience? (Who will watch this video?)
  - young children in school
  - young people who like to travel
  - teachers
  - ESL students
3. What is the purpose of this video?
  - to teach children about farmer
  - to persuade people to become vegans
  - to model an interview for English Language students
4. Would you like to have a job like Andy's? Why or why not?
5. Tell your partner something interesting you learned from this video.
6. If you interviewed a friend or family member about their job, what questions would you ask?
  - Write a list of questions here.
  - Then share your questions with your partner. Add some good questions from your partner's list.
