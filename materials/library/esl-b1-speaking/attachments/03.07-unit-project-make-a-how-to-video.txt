Organizing a How To Video: Answers
| What is this video about? | This video is about how to make zero waste toothpaste - toothpaste that doesn’t produce any trash such as the toothpaste tube. |
| What are the benefits of learning to do this? | Toothpaste tubes are difficult if not impossible to recycle. This toothpaste works extremely well. It’s quick and easy to make. |
| What tools or ingredients do you need? | 2 tablespoons of organic coconut oil 1 tablespoon of baking soda fifteen to twenty drops of organic essential oil (for flavor) a bowl and something to stir it together |
| What are the steps? | combine the coconut oil and baking soda and add the essential oil for flavor mix it together use a spoon to scoop a small portion of the paste onto your toothbrush brush you teeth |
| What tips, helpful advice, or important information was included? | it doesn’t foam up like normal toothpaste it might be a little saltier than you’re used to If the paste is too hard, put it under some hot water for a few minutes If it’s too liquid, put it in the freezer for a little while |
| How does the video end? | She says “now you’re ready to make ___” “thank you so much for watching? She provides her website and youtube channel in case you want more info |
