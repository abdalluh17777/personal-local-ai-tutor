## Simple Present Tense Practice
Practice with the simple present tense. Fill in the blanks with the correct form of the simple present tense.
The Mission District in San Francisco, California _________ (have) many immigrants from Mexico, Latin America, and Central America. People from these countries _________ (speak) Spanish, so they are sometimes called Hispanic. The people who live in the Mission District _________ (keep) their cultural identity by sharing their food, religion, art, and music. The music ____________ (allow) them to experience their griefs and joys--that is, the things that make them sad and happy. The artists __________ (create) public art that is outside where everyone ______________ (see) it and _____________ (enjoy) it. The video _____________ (show) that the Mission District is a good example of integration, where the old culture and new culture _____________ (mix) peacefully.

## Compound Sentences

After two weeks in Spain, Caitlin understood that adjusting to a new culture takes time. ## Subjects and Verbs
- Know how to find the subject and the verb of a sentence.
- The **subject** is the person, place, or thing doing an action.
- The **verb** shows the action or "being."
## Practice: Identify the subject and verb. There may be more than one subject or more than one verb.
- My friends and I are going to the movies after class.
- We want to see the new Spider-man movie.
- Spider-man is our favorite superhero.
- The movie theater at the mall is cheaper.
- I like to eat popcorn, and sometimes I also buy a soda.
## Active and Stative Verbs
- Know the difference between simple present and present continuous/progressive
## Practice: Choose simple present or present continuous in the sentences below.
- We ________________ (have) dinner at my grandmother's house every Sunday.
- Tonight, she _____________ (cook) vegetarian pasta because my sister ________________ (not/eat) meat.
- Pasta ______________ (be) my favorite food.
- Right now, I ______________ (make) some salad to bring to her house.
- My sister usually ______________ (bring) dessert.
- I ___________ (be) so excited for dinner.
## Common and Proper Nouns
- Know the difference between common nouns and proper nouns.
- Common nouns are people, places, or things in general.
- Proper nouns are people, places, or things specifically; you should capitalize the first letter of a proper noun.
## Practice: Change the capitalization of the proper nouns. Some sentences have no proper nouns.
- We love going to the mountains in the winter months.
- This january we will go to big bear mountain.
- My grandchildren go to lexington elementary school.
- That elementary school is close to my house.
## Compound Sentences
- Know how to write compound sentences
- Compound sentences have two independent clauses connected by a comma and a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS).
- The most common coordinating conjunctions are "and," "but," "or," and "so."
## Practice: Write your own compound sentences about a family member or a close friend. Use "and," but," "or," and "so" at least one time each.
## Adjective Word Order
- Know how to use adjectives in order.
## Practice
Write a paragraph using adjectives to describe the favorite thing in your house. Use at least one compound sentence. Practice using simple present and present progressive verbs. When you finish, identify all the verbs in your paragraph. Next, give your paper to a partner. Check your verb tenses together.
