# Lesson 15: Listening and Speaking: An Interview About a Job

*English Stage 1 (B1) · Unit 2: Getting to Know You*
*Adapted from Speaking, Listening and Pronunciation Projects for ELLs - Intermediate Level by Brittany Zemlick, Grossmont College (CC BY 4.0): 2.1: Listening and Speaking- Interview - Andy's Job; 2.2: Vocabulary Review.*

## In this lesson
You will watch a short video interview with Andy, a farmer who cares about the environment. You will learn words about farming and the environment, listen for the main idea and then for details, and write the questions an interviewer asks about a person's job.

## Learning goals
After this lesson you should be able to:
- Use words about farming and the environment, such as "plant crops", "harvest" and "sustainable".
- Understand the main idea and the details of a short interview.
- Name the genre, audience and purpose of a video.
- Ask and answer questions about a person's job, daily tasks and values.

## Key language
| Word or phrase | Meaning in simple English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| sustainable | can continue for a long time; it is not used up or destroyed | Windmills and solar panels are sustainable energy sources. |
| sustainable agriculture | farming that does not damage the environment, so we can grow food in the future | Andy practices sustainable agriculture. |
| industrial agriculture | farming with very large numbers of plants and animals, to sell for food | This kind of farming can damage the environment. |
| values | the things that are most important to you in life | Your values can include family, money, education, music or art. |
| plant crops | put seeds in the ground so they grow into food | Andy plants crops on his farm. |
| harvest | pick the fruits and vegetables when they are ready | Andy picks the vegetables on his farm: he harvests them. |
| produce (noun) | fresh fruits and vegetables | Fresh vegetables are produce. |
| challenging | difficult, but maybe interesting or enjoyable | Is your job challenging? |
| pollute | make water, air or land dirty | Trash, oil and chemicals pollute the water. |

## Rules and explanations

### Listening in two steps
- First for the **main idea** (the big picture): watch once without stopping.
- Then for **details** (names, places, numbers): watch again, and stop and replay when you need to.

### Genre, audience and purpose
- **Genre**: what kind of video it is (an interview, a movie, a presentation...).
- **Audience**: who will watch it.
- **Purpose**: why someone made it (to teach, to persuade, to show an example...).

### Interview questions about a job
The detail questions are about Andy ("he"). An interviewer asks the same questions with "you":

| About Andy | To the person |
|---|---|
| Where did he grow up? | Where did you grow up? |
| Where does he live now? | Where do you live now? |
| What does he do every day? | What do you do every day? |
| Why does he like his job? | Why do you like your job? |
| Does he have any pets? | Do you have any pets? |

- Present simple questions: **does** with he/she/it, **do** with I/you/we/they. The main verb stays in the base form: "What does he do?" (not "What does he does?").
- Short answers: "Yes, he does." / "No, he doesn't."
- Past questions: **did** + base verb: "Where did he grow up?" (not "grew").

> **Note for Arabic speakers**
> - Arabic questions do not use "do/does/did". In English you need them: "Where does he live now?" (not "Where he lives now?").
> - Arabic has no /p/ sound. Practise /p/ as in "pen" and /b/ as in "bag" with "plant", "pull", "pickup", "produce" and "public".

## Practice

**1. Before you watch.** Answer aloud in 2 or 3 sentences: a) Andy is a farmer. What do you think he does every day? b) Andy practices sustainable agriculture. What is sustainable agriculture? If you don't know, guess. c) What are your values?

**2. Vocabulary.** Task 2 in your material uses pictures. Instead, write the word for each definition from that table. Choose from: pull weeds, challenging, vegan, meat production, destroy, habitat, podcast.
1. raising animals for food
2. remove unwanted plants
3. a radio or music program you can download to your phone or computer from the internet
4. something that is difficult but may be interesting or enjoyable
5. a place where animals live
6. to cause something to end or no longer exist
7. a person who does not eat food that comes from animals (milk, meat, eggs)

**3. Watch for the main idea.** Watch the video once (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wa0582oOYgY) and take notes. What does Andy do every day in his job? What are his values?

**4. Listen for detail (book interactive exercise).** Watch again and choose.
1. What's the man's name? ________
2. Where did he grow up? Oxford / San Diego / El Cajon
3. Where does he live now? Oxford / San Diego / Scotland
4. How many brothers and sisters does he have? 1 / 2 / 3
5. Where are his parents from? England and Poland / El Cajon and San Diego / Scotland and Poland
6. What's his job? He's a farmer. / He's a teacher. / He's a vegan.
7. What does he do every day? He waters plants. / He pulls weeds. / He plants crops. / He repairs fences. / All of the answers are correct.
8. What does he like about his job? Choose all correct answers: (a) being outdoors in a beautiful place (b) working to improve the environment (c) office work (d) working with the public
9. What doesn't he like about his job? Choose all correct answers from (a) to (d).
10. Which one is NOT his hobby? cooking / running / playing the guitar / listening to podcasts / riding his bike
11. Does he have any pets? Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. / He lives in a house with two cats.
12. What kind of car does he drive? a pickup truck / a Toyota Camry / a Jeep
13. Does he like waking up early in the morning? Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
14. Why doesn't he eat meat? He doesn't like the taste of meat. / He feels that eating meat hurts the environment. / He eats meat every day.
15. What are the most important things in his life? His family and making the world a better place / Running and playing music / Family, money, and cooking

**5. After you watch.** Choose and say why.
a) Genre: a TikTok video / a movie / a presentation / an interview
b) Most likely audience: young children in school / young people who like to travel / teachers / ESL students
c) Purpose: to teach children about farmers / to persuade people to become vegans / to model an interview for English language students
d) Would you like to have a job like Andy's? Why or why not? Record your answer and listen back.

**6. Your interview questions.** If you interviewed a friend or family member about their job, what questions would you ask? Write at least 8. Then ask your tutor for 3 more good questions and add them to your list.

**7. Vocabulary review A (book).** Use: sustainable, industrial agriculture, values, plant crops, pull weeds, water, harvest, pickup truck, repair, produce. Spell correctly.
1. I need to ____ the plants in the garden. It's very dry and hot today.
2. Andy drives a ____. He uses it for his farming business.
3. Many people buy ____ from the grocery store, but you can also grow fruits and vegetables at home.
4. Wind energy is ____ because we can use it forever and it will never run out.
5. Farmers usually ____ in the spring. They put seeds in the ground and wait for them to grow into food.
6. Farmers usually ____ crops in the fall. They pick the fruits and vegetables and then eat them or sell them.
7. In the video, Andy talks about his ____. The most important things in his life are family and protecting the environment.
8. If you ____ something, that means you're fixing it.
9. ____ means growing large amounts of vegetables, fruit, and animals, usually in a relatively small space.
10. Gardeners ____ (remove plants they don't want) to make room for the plants they want to grow.

**8. Vocabulary review B (book).** Use: podcasts, challenging, vegan, environmental impact, meat production, pollutes, carbon, habitats, fences.
1. A ____ is a person who doesn't eat meat or use animal products.
2. Andy says working with the public can be ____.
3. I like to listen to ____ on my iPhone.
4. Industrial agriculture is bad for the environment because it ____ waterways.
5. If we destroy the ____ of animals, they won't have a place to live.
6. Industrial agriculture and some forms of energy production release ____ into the atmosphere.
7. ____ is the process of raising animals to use them for food.
8. Many people are concerned about the ____ of airplanes.
9. Farmers have to put ____ around their crops so that animals won't eat them.

## Speak with your tutor
- "You are Andy, the farmer from the video. I will interview you about your job, your family and your values. Answer, and tell me if one of my questions has a mistake."
- "Interview me about my job or my studies. Ask one question at a time, and ask one follow-up question after each answer."
- "Here are my 8 questions to interview a friend about their job. Check the grammar of each one and tell me which questions are the best and why."
- "Let me retell Andy's interview in 6 to 8 sentences. Correct my mistakes, especially does and doesn't."
