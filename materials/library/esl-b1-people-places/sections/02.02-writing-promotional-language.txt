## Use these activities to analyze and practice the promotional language in the tourist blurb “Welcome to East County.”
## This lesson is available as a worksheet.
Find the following phrases in the blurb "__Welcome to East County__" and highlight them. Then complete the sentences on this worksheet.
- The area offers ____________
- Spend an afternoon ________
- Don't miss ________________
- ___________ is famous for _____________
Write the grammatical structure that follows each of these phrases...
- The area offers + _____________
- Spend an afternoon + _________
- Don't miss + ________________
- __________ is famous for + ______________
Write two sentences about each of the following places. Start each sentence with one of the four phrases above.
[Image: old town.jpg]
- Old Town, San Diego, CA
  - EX: Old Town offers shopping, historical sites, and great restaurants.
[Image: ocean beach.jpg]
- Ocean Beach, CA
  - EX: Spend an afternoon surfing the waves, throwing a ball for your pooch at dog's beach, or sampling excellent vegan food at one of Ocean Beach's excellent cafes.
[Image: Little italy.jpg]
- Little Italy, San Diego, CA
  - Don't miss the weekly farmer's market every Saturday 8am - 2pm.
Look back at the "Welcome to East County" webpage. Are there any other phrases you could use in your own tourist blurb?
