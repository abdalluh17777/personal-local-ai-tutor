## Watching a News Report: El Cajon, California
## Pre-Watch
Skim the photos in the article, __Baghdad on the Border__, and answer the question below...
- What aspects of their cultural identity have Chaldean Iraqis brought to their new home? Make a list. You may use vocabulary from the previous section, or add your own.
## While You Watch
Watch the video, __"Thousands of Iraqi Christians Make El Cajon California Home"__, and answer the questions below...
- Where is this news report published? What is the name of the website?
- What is the purpose of this news report? Choose one
  - To convince people that Chaldean immigrants help improve El Cajon
  - To explain what is special about Chaldean immigrants
  - To convince people that they should move to El Cajon
  - To convince people that they should vacation in El Cajon
- Does this video show the integration of Chaldean Iraqis in El Cajon, CA? What parts of the video help you decide the answer to this question?
## Discuss or Write
These photos and videos show the many aspects of their first culture that immigrants can bring to a new home, such as art, music, food, and religion. They continue to practice these aspects of their culture. Consider your own experience. What aspects of your culture have you brought to the United States? Will you continue to practice those aspects of your culture?
## Vocabulary Definitions
- Culture (n): The way of life of a group of people. Culture includes art, music, religion, language, food, tradition, and many other aspects. The adjective form is "cultural"
- Immigrant (n): A person who comes to live permanently in a foreign country
- Grief (n): A feeling of sadness or something that causes sadness
- Joy (n): A feeling of happiness or something that causes happiness
- Integration (n): When two cultures mix (blend, combine) in a society and both cultures have equal value
- Hispanic (adj): Coming from a Spanish-speaking country
- Public (adj): Relating to or open to all people in an area
