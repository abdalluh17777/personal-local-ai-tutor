## Simple Present Tense Practice
Practice with the simple present tense. Fill in the blanks with the correct form of the simple present tense.
The Mission District in San Francisco, California _________ (have) many immigrants from Mexico, Latin America, and Central America. People from these countries _________ (speak) Spanish, so they are sometimes called Hispanic. The people who live in the Mission District _________ (keep) their cultural identity by sharing their food, religion, art, and music. The music ____________ (allow) them to experience their griefs and joys--that is, the things that make them sad and happy. The artists __________ (create) public art that is outside where everyone ______________ (see) it and _____________ (enjoy) it. The video _____________ (show) that the Mission District is a good example of integration, where the old culture and new culture _____________ (mix) peacefully.

## Compound Sentences
Revisit the last paragraph of "Overcoming Culture Shock," below. Can you find two compound sentences? Identify the FANBOYS in each sentence.
After two weeks in Spain, Caitlin understood that adjusting to a new culture takes time. It can take weeks or months to recover from culture shock, and it can take years to fully adjust to living in a new culture. During her trip, she made new lifelong friends. She learned a lot about Spain, but she also discovered a lot about herself and her own culture
Are the sentences below compound? If not, why not? Identify the verbs, subjects, and FANBOYS.
- It was exciting, but she felt alone and uncomfortable
- Maria's mother kissed Caitlin on both cheeks, and Caitlin was surprised
- People feel incompetent and nervous because of culture shock
- She felt overwhelmed by the new customs, language, and surroundings
- There was new food, new daily schedules, and new rules of etiquette
- They spoke loudly and they argued about politics
- That night, Caitlin crawled into a strange bed, but she wanted to go home
- We relate to others through shared cultural norms, and ordinarily, we don't even notice them
Compound sentences use "and," "or," "but," and "so" to connect two clauses. Write one sentence on each line. Then write two compound sentences of your own. Remember to use a comma before the FANBOYS...

The area offers most beautiful beaches in the world. spend an afternoon swimming or surfing at Del Mar Beach, or go hiking at Torrey Pines State Park. Remember to take a picnic and enjoy the sound of the waves after your hike. The weather here is more warm than other cities, and it's always fun to eat outside.
