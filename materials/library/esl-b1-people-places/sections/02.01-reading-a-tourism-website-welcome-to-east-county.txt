## Reading

## Reading Questions
**After you read, answer these questions to check your understanding.**
- What is the title of the web page? Where did you find the title?
- What location is the web page describing?
- Who might like to read this web page? (the audience)
  - Children in elementary school
  - Tourists who want to travel to Southern California
  - Immigrants who want to move to San Diego
  - Politicians who want to understand the problems in the area
- What is the purpose of the web page? In other words, what does the author want the reader to do?
  - Explain why East County is a good place to live
  - Give information about East County
  - Convince the reader to visit East County on vacation
  - Give the history of East County
- How did you decide the purpose of the web page? In other words, what parts of the page tell you its purpose?
## Understanding and Using Adjectives and Noun Modifiers
## Form: Adjective + Noun Combinations
**Identify the adjective + noun combinations in the following sentence.**
"*San Diego's East County wilderness is one of the most diverse areas in the county featuring rolling hills, majestic mountains, sparkling lakes, deep canyons, and desert landscapes plus Indian casino gaming*."
Adjectives: diverse, rolling, majestic, sparkling, deep, desert
Nouns: areas, hills, mountains, lakes, canyons, landscapes.
- What is the rule for adjective + noun word order?
  - DETERMINER + ADJECTIVE + NOUN
  - Examples: a sparkling lake, some rolling hills, the majestic mountains
## Form: Noun Modifiers
**In this sentence, which word tells us 'what kind of sports?'**
People use lakes for water sports.
Both the word __ ***water*** __ and the word __ ***sports*** __are nouns. But __ ***water*** __ is telling us __what kind__ of __ ***sports*** __.
In this phrase, __ ***sports*** __is the __head (or main) noun__, and __ ***water*** __ is the __noun modifier.__
**Find the noun and noun modifier in this sentence.**
San Diego has many beautiful golf courses.
golf= noun modifier
courses= head noun
## Meaning: Adjectives and Noun Modifiers
**Read the two paragraphs below. Which paragraph is more interesting? Which paragraph makes you want to visit East County? Why? Discuss with your classmates and instructor.**
- In addition to outdoor adventures, East San Diego County boasts plenty of entertainment. Sizzling Las Vegas-style Native American casinos, luxurious resorts tucked right into the rugged landscape, challenging golf courses built around natural obstacles, historic sites and highways, and annual festivals and rodeos can be enjoyed, all within the expansive landscape of this California wilderness country.
- In addition to adventures, East San Diego County has entertainment. Casinos, resorts, golf courses, site and highways, and festivals and rodeos can be enjoyed, all within the landscape of this country.
**In the first paragraph above, identify the words that are missing in the second paragraph**
- Why do we use adjectives in a composition? Choose all that apply...
  - To add interesting details
  - To convince the reader that something is good or bad
  - To help the reader make a picture in their mind
  - Other (please specify)
- What is the usual word order for adjective and noun combinations?
  - (Article) + adjective + noun
  - (Article) + noun + adjective
