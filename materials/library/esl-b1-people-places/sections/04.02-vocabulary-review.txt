## Vocabulary from Listening Activities
This page includes a list of target vocabulary for each activity in the module "People and Places." Complete each task to practice using the vocabulary.
## Sightseeing
- Guide (n)
- Art museum (n)
- Restaurant (n)
- Downtown (n)
- Zoo (n)
- Souvenir (n)
- Subway (n)
- Seashore (n)
- Beach (n)
- Recommend (v)
## Practice
Answer these questions with your classmates.
- What restaurant do you recommend to your classmates?
- Read the following vocabulary words out loud. Does your city have one? Where is it?
  - Art museum
  - Zoo
  - Subway
  - Beach
  - Seashore
- Have you ever brought a souvenir home from your travels? What was it?
- Where can you find a city guide to your city? Do you need a city guide when you go downtown in your city?

## Practice
Answer these questions with your classmates.
- Use the vocabulary above. Do you enjoy any of the activities on the list? Why or why not?
- Where is a lake near your city?
- If you could only live near a beach or a mountain, which would you choose? Why?
- Read the vocabulary below. Have you ever been to one of these? Did you enjoy it?
  - Golf course
  - Casino
  - Rodeo
  - Resort
  - Art gallery
- What is the difference between an art gallery and an art museum?

## Vocabulary from Textbook Reading "Overcoming Culture Shock"
Use complete sentences to write the answer to these questions.
- What are several examples of taboos in your culture?
- Do people make a toast in your culture? If so, what do they say before they drink?
- What facial expressions do people make when they are happy? Angry?
- Give an example of a time you felt overwhelmed
- What are three customs you follow from your culture?
- Have you experienced culture shock? What did it feel like?
- What are some things people can do to adjust quickly to a new culture?
- What is one example of a nonverbal gesture?
