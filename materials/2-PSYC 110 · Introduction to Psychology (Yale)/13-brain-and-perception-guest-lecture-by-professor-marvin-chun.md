# Guest Lecture 1: Brain and Perception

*Introduction to Psychology · Yale University, Spring 2007 · Professor Marvin Chun*
*Study guide based on the official course overview. Source: https://oyc.yale.edu/psychology/psyc-110/guest-lecture-1 (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).*

## Before you start
In this guest lecture, Professor Marvin Chun gave an overview of how the brain works. He used one example: how people recognize faces and tell them apart. He also described the main methods scientists use to study the brain.

## Learning goals
After this lecture you should be able to:
- Name the four methods for studying brain function listed in the overview.
- Describe what scientists have discovered about face processing in the brain.
- Explain what causes face blindness.

## Key ideas
### 1. Brain function and faces
The lecture gave an overview of brain function, using the specific example of how people recognize and discriminate (tell apart) faces. It also gave a basic summary of how neurons (brain cells) work and how the brain is organized.

### 2. Methods for studying the brain
The overview lists four methods that scientists use:
- psychophysics
- single-cell electrophysiology
- functional brain imaging
- neuropsychology

### 3. Special mechanisms for faces
Using these methods, scientists have discovered that the brain contains special mechanisms dedicated to face processing. Damage to these brain regions causes face blindness.
